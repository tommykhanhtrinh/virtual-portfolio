ENGINEERING QUESTIONS / 02

What are we trying to prove?

The page will evolve around questions, experiments, and decisions rather than only finished mechanisms.

01 · MECHANICAL

Can the drivetrain stay consistent?

Motor distribution, alignment, traction, weight balance, and mechanical reliability affect every autonomous result downstream.

02 · LOCALIZATION

Where does the robot think it is?

Odometry, IMU, rotation sensors, and future sensor fusion work are being treated as a localization system rather than isolated sensors.

03 · TESTING

Can we measure repeatability?

Timing, pressure behavior, path error, mechanism consistency, and competition conditions need repeatable tests before design decisions are made.

04 · TEAM PROCESS

Can everyone explain why the robot changed?

Robot history, version decisions, sensor choices, mechanism trade-offs, and competition lessons should not live in one person's head. The goal is shared engineering context across builders, programmers, strategists, and drivers.

05 · LEADERSHIP

Delegate the system, not just tasks.

My role is increasingly about setting clear ownership, review points, evidence, and decision criteria so the team can move without constant micromanagement.
