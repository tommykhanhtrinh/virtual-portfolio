LSTS × FU JEN CATHOLIC UNIVERSITY

Career Summer Camp & Coding Summer Camp

Across two programs in Kon Tum, I taught English, Scratch programming, PowerPoint, presentation skills, and foundational soft skills to approximately 200 elementary and middle school students. In 2025, I also served as Vice Camp Leader, coordinated operations and media, supported translation, and worked with Fu Jen students and professors.

WHY IT MATTERED

The goal was not just to “teach software,” but to help students feel that technology was something they could touch, understand, and create with.

VIETNAM ROBOTICS AMBASSADOR

FIRST Global Challenge Team Vietnam

As Southern Region Innovation Department Leader, I helped deliver Open STEM Day and Think Bootcamp at FPT Hau Giang School, integrating fire-prevention STEM workshops with hands-on robotics experiences.

CROSS-CULTURAL EXCHANGE
