Two summers.
One evolving idea of service.

From syntax to confidence.

THE BRIDGE / 02

I returned to the same place — but not in the same role.

That transition is the story. 2024 taught me to zoom in: read one learner, simplify one idea, debug one explanation. 2025 taught me to zoom out: design the conditions so many learners and many teams could move together.
