ENGINEERING STORY / 02

What broke the “just build it” mindset.

My first season made design trade-offs visible. When a robot is already assembled, even a small drivetrain or mechanism change can cascade through the entire structure.

01 · BUILD

Freestyle first.

We moved quickly without a complete CAD/BOM workflow, which made early construction flexible but later changes harder to coordinate.

02 · TEST

Problems became structural.

Reliability issues were not always isolated mechanism failures; sometimes they traced back to drivetrain alignment, motor distribution, or how the robot had been assembled.

03 · LEARN

Plan before cutting metal.

The season pushed me toward a more deliberate process: CAD, BOM planning, testing criteria, version history, and evidence-based revision.

REFLECTION / 05

I stopped treating the robot as a collection of parts.

By the end of the season, I was beginning to see it as an engineering system: geometry, structure, mechanisms, electronics, code, documentation, driver behavior, and testing all affect one another. That became the foundation for the next season.
