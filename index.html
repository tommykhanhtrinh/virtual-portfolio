ABOUT ME / 01

Robotics taught me how to think.
Service taught me why it matters.

01

First, make it move.

Competition robotics made engineering immediate. A mechanism either worked or it didn’t. Across two VEX seasons, I learned to build, test, diagnose, and rebuild — using every match and every failure as data for the next version.

02

Then, make the team move.

As a team captain and Robotics Club president, the challenge became bigger than the robot. I had to align people, decisions, documentation, practice, and deadlines — without becoming the person who does everything.

03

Finally, make it useful to someone else.

Teaching younger students and running STEM activities changed what I considered a successful project. The best technical knowledge is not something I keep on a competition field; it is something I can explain, share, and use to help someone else become more confident.

“Build something that works. Then make what you learned travel further than the robot.”
